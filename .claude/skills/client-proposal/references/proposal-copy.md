## Credibility paragraph — do NOT add a production-credit list
The paragraph above is the full, standardized version. Do not append or reintroduce
an artist/production-credit list (e.g. "with production credits including Curren$y,
Wiz Khalifa, NoCap, Zaytoven, and Boosie") — this was removed as of PRO-2026-018 and
confirmed removed again as of PRO-2026-022. If a client's project is more music/artist
oriented, adjust "working businesses" to "working artists and businesses" and swap the
booking/payment clause for a content-fit one — but never add the credit list back.

## Tone rules
- Plain, confident, zero hype words ("revolutionary", "cutting-edge" are banned)
- Outcomes over features: "ready to take money", "booked while you sleep"
- Short sentences. New Orleans direct.
- Never publish internal numbers (other clients' prices, revenue) in a proposal.
